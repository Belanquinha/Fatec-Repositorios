- (direction) Roles in scope: student, teacher, system admin; gestor role is explicitly out of scope
- (decision) Gestor role removed: no institutional gestor login, no institution screen as a role workspace; reason: minimize secretariat/gestor workload (orientation request)
- (decision) Student selects the responsible professor at posting time so the project has an immediate owner instead of floating loose to the institution
- (decision) Hard cap of 4 institutions per professor to stop one professor from selecting everything and concentrating the system
- (decision) Institutions are catalog data (official pre-populated list, imported or manually curated with official data); maintained by the system admin, not by secretariat; professor never free-types an institution
- (decision) Professor validation happens by email domain (@cps read from MSAL preferred_username/UPN); there is no separate professor list - the @cps domain IS the list
- (decision) Login must authenticate against the CPS tenant only (MSAL restricted to single tenant); otherwise any Microsoft account could log in as a student
- (question) Confirm in practice that the @cps domain is exclusive to professors (no student, intern, secretariat, or direction uses it); if it breaks, the classification rule needs a second validation
- (question) Define explicitly who approves a project: only the responsible professor (recommended) or any professor of the institution
- (question) Define the owner of moderation/disputes (undue project, wrong link); for now 'nobody', recorded as a conscious decision
- (capability) CAP-1: Login via MSAL restricted to the single CPS tenant (student and professor authenticate with CPS Microsoft accounts)
- (capability) CAP-2: Backend-centralized role classification by email domain: @cps UPN -> professor, any other CPS tenant member -> student
- (capability) CAP-3: Professor selects 1 to 4 institutions from the official pre-populated catalog (no free-text registration)
- (capability) CAP-4: Student posts a project and selects the responsible professor at posting time
- (capability) CAP-5: Professor(s) approve projects linked to their selected institution(s)
- (note) Roles matrix (student/teacher/admin + removed gestor) is a multi-item catalog: held in companion roles.md
